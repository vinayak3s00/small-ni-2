# AbetVoice — Multilingual AI Voice & Telephony Agents

> **Product:** AbetVoice · voice.abetworks.in
> **Tier 2.4 · Strong adjacency** — The one product category Abetworks doesn't visibly have yet. Voice is the hottest AI infra lane in 2026.
> **Positioning:** "Your best rep, on every call, in every language — 24/7."

---

## 1. The opportunity

Voice AI is exploding, especially in healthcare (voice-AI healthcare market ~$468M in 2024 → ~$3.18B by 2030). Indian businesses run on phone calls, and most miss or mishandle them. AbetVoice adds voice to the suite, sharing the same record, permissions, and governance.

## 2. What AbetVoice does

1. **Inbound** — answers calls, authenticates the caller, resolves the request (booking, status, FAQ), escalates cleanly with a summary.
2. **Outbound** — speed-to-lead callbacks (<60s), reminders, follow-ups, surveys.
3. **Multilingual** — Hindi, Marathi, English, extensible to regional languages.
4. **Grounded + cited** — answers only from approved sources; logs and transcribes everything into the one record.
5. **Governed** — same field-level permissions and audit trail as the rest of the suite (critical for healthcare/fintech).

## 3. Why Abetworks wins

Competitors (Vapi, Retell, ElevenLabs) sell voice **infrastructure**. AbetVoice sells voice **inside a governed business suite** — the call updates the CRM, respects permissions, and is audit-logged, in Indian languages. That vertical + governance + language combination is hard to copy.

## 4. Go-to-market

- **ICP:** clinics/diagnostics (appointment lines), real estate (speed-to-lead callbacks), support teams (tier-1 deflection).
- **Motion:** attach to existing suite customers first (AbetCare, AbetRealty), then standalone.
- **Pricing:** per-minute + per-concurrent-line + platform fee.

## 5. Build phases

1. Telephony + real-time media (LiveKit/WebRTC + SIP trunk) and STT/TTS pipeline.
2. Barge-in, turn-taking, low-latency orchestration.
3. Multilingual (Hindi/Marathi) tuning + accent robustness.
4. CRM write-back + cited answers + governed escalation.
5. WhatsApp voice notes + call-to-chat continuity.

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Latency/quality of Indian-language voice | Streaming STT/TTS, regional model tuning, human fallback |
| Telephony/regulatory (TRAI/DND) | DND checks, consent, licensed SIP providers |
| Commoditized infra competitors | Sell governance + vertical + CRM integration, not raw voice |
