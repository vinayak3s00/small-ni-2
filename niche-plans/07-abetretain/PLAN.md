# AbetRetain — Retention & CX AI for D2C / E-commerce

> **Product:** AbetRetain · retain.abetworks.in
> **Tier 3.7 · Opportunistic** — Post-purchase WhatsApp journeys, order-support automation, and retention programmes for D2C brands and marketplaces.
> **Positioning:** "Turn one order into a lifetime — on WhatsApp."

---

## 1. The opportunity

D2C brands over-spend on acquisition and under-invest in retention. Order support is reactive and manual, and post-purchase communication is generic. WhatsApp is the highest-engagement channel in India for exactly this.

## 2. What AbetRetain does

1. **Post-purchase journeys** — order confirmation, shipping, delivery, review, replenishment/upsell on WhatsApp.
2. **Order-support automation** — WISMO ("where is my order"), returns, exchanges resolved autonomously with citations, escalating cleanly.
3. **Retention programmes** — win-back, loyalty, replenishment reminders driven by purchase signals.
4. **Explainable churn/LTV scoring** — which customers to save and why.
5. **One record** — support + marketing + orders on the same customer profile.

## 3. Why Abetworks wins

Autonomous, cited support resolution + one data model means support headcount stays flat as orders scale (a documented Abetworks outcome). WhatsApp-first fits Indian D2C.

## 4. Go-to-market

- **ICP:** D2C brands and marketplaces (₹1–100 Cr GMV) on Shopify/WooCommerce/custom.
- **Wedge:** "Resolve two-thirds of support on WhatsApp, then sell them again."
- **Pricing:** per-conversation + per-order-volume tier + platform fee.

## 5. Build phases

1. E-commerce connectors (Shopify/WooCommerce/marketplace webhooks).
2. Post-purchase journey engine on AbetFlow.
3. Order-support agent (WISMO/returns/exchanges) with cited answers.
4. Churn/LTV scoring + retention programmes.
5. Loyalty/replenishment automations.

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Platform (Shopify) dependency | Multi-connector + webhook resilience |
| WhatsApp over-messaging fatigue | Frequency caps + opt-in + value-first cadence |
| Support accuracy | Cited answers, confidence-gated escalation |
