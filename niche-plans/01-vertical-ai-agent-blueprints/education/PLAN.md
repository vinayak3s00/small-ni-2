# Education / EdTech Admissions Agent

> **Vertical:** Education & EdTech (schools, colleges, coaching institutes, EdTech platforms)
> **Flagship outcome:** Higher admissions conversion through instant applicant response, counsellor productivity, and parent communication in the family's language.

---

## 1. The problem

Admissions is a high-intent, time-boxed funnel that most institutions run badly:

- Enquiries from ads, referrals, and walk-ins scatter across counsellors' phones and notebooks.
- Follow-up depends on individual counsellor diligence; hot applicants go cold over a weekend.
- **Parents** — the real decision-makers — are under-communicated with, and often in Hindi/Marathi rather than English.
- No visibility into counsellor productivity or cohort-level conversion.

## 2. The agent (what it does)

1. **Captures** every admission enquiry across WhatsApp, calls, web, and ad forms into one record.
2. **Responds instantly** with course/program info, fees, eligibility, and next steps.
3. **Qualifies and scores** applicants (intent, eligibility fit, financing) with **explainable reasoning** counsellors can correct.
4. **Nurtures parents** with scheduled, multilingual updates (Hindi/Marathi/English) — deadlines, documents, counselling slots.
5. **Books counselling sessions and campus visits**, with reminders and reschedules.
6. **Reports** counsellor productivity and cohort conversion for leadership.

## 3. Domain data model

- **Record types:** Applicant, Parent/Guardian, Program/Course, Counselling Session, Application, Cohort.
- **Pipeline stages:** Enquiry → Contacted → Qualified → Counselling Booked → Applied → Document/Fee → Enrolled.
- **Key fields:** program of interest, eligibility data, financing need, parent contact + language, counsellor owner, cohort/intake.

## 4. Compliance profile

- Consent for marketing to applicants **and** parents; DND-aware.
- Minor-appropriate handling — parent/guardian is the primary contact where the applicant is a minor.
- Audit trail of commitments made (fees, scholarships) to prevent misselling disputes.
- Data residency + retention aligned to DPDP obligations.

## 5. Channel & language pack

- WhatsApp-first + voice; **Hindi & Marathi** parent-communication templates as a first-class feature (extensible to regional languages).
- Deadline reminders, document checklists, fee/scholarship updates, counselling confirmations.

## 6. Benchmark metrics (publish medians, disclose weakest case)

| Metric | Target signal |
|--------|---------------|
| Enquiry-to-counselling booking rate | +20–35% |
| First-response time | < 1 minute |
| Parent engagement (message read/response) | measurable lift |
| Counsellor leads worked per day | +30% via automation of repetitive follow-up |

## 7. Go-to-market

- **ICP:** coaching institutes and colleges with 500–10,000 annual enquiries; multi-branch schools; EdTech with counsellor sales motions.
- **Wedge:** "Talk to every parent, in their language, before your competitor calls back."
- **Proof:** 3 design partners across a coaching chain, a college, and an EdTech; publish conversion lift.
- **Pricing:** per-counsellor seat + per-intake campaign module; blueprint deployment for multi-branch groups.

## 8. 12-week build plan

1. Weeks 1–2: 5 institution design partners; map admissions funnel + parent-comms needs.
2. Weeks 3–5: applicant/parent data model, ad-form + WhatsApp ingestion, Hindi/Marathi templates.
3. Weeks 6–8: qualification scoring, parent-nurture automations, counselling booking, cohort reporting.
4. Weeks 9–10: pilots across intake season; measure booking + conversion lift.
5. Weeks 11–12: packaging, launch (timed to an admissions cycle).

## 9. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Seasonality (intake windows) | Time launch to admissions cycle; annual + seasonal pricing |
| Minor-data sensitivity | Parent-primary contact model; consent + audit |
| Misselling disputes | Log every fee/scholarship commitment |
| Counsellor resistance | Position as productivity tool that feeds them warm, scored leads |
