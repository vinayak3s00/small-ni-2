# AbetConcierge — WhatsApp-first AI Concierge for Indian SMBs

> **Product:** AbetConcierge · concierge.abetworks.in
> **Tier 1.3 · Highest fit** — The "graduation path" for SMBs who outgrow Meta's built-in Business Agent and shallow WhatsApp tools (Wati, AiSensy, Interakt, Gallabox).
> **Positioning:** "Start on WhatsApp. Grow into a real revenue engine."

---

## 1. The market gap

Meta's WhatsApp Business Agent (launched in India, 2026) and WhatsApp-native tools own the shallow end: fast to deploy, cheap, but limited in AI depth and cross-channel capability. SMBs hit a ceiling:
- the bot answers FAQs but there's no CRM, no attribution, no pipeline behind it;
- WhatsApp is siloed from calls, email, and Instagram;
- no GST-aware quoting, multi-currency, or explainable lead scoring.

## 2. What AbetConcierge is

A WhatsApp-first AI concierge with a **real revenue engine** underneath:

1. **Conversational commerce** — answers, recommends, quotes (GST-aware), and books/sells 24/7.
2. **One data model** — every WhatsApp chat becomes a CRM record with history, owner, and pipeline stage.
3. **Cross-channel** — the same customer on WhatsApp, Instagram, call, and email is one record.
4. **Explainable scoring** — tells the SMB which leads are hot and why.
5. **GST-aware quoting + multi-currency** — built for how India sells.
6. **Graduation path** — starts free/cheap, upgrades into full NuCRM + AbetFlow without a migration.

## 4. Go-to-market

- **ICP:** Indian SMBs (retail, D2C, services, clinics, coaching) doing 100–5,000 WhatsApp conversations/month.
- **Wedge:** free tier / low entry price; land on WhatsApp, expand into CRM + automation.
- **Motion:** product-led (self-serve) + partner/reseller channel.
- **Pricing:** free starter → per-conversation + per-seat; upsell to suite.

## 5. Build phases

1. WhatsApp Business API (BSP) integration + conversational commerce agent.
2. Auto-CRM record creation + explainable scoring.
3. GST-aware quoting + multi-currency + catalog.
4. Cross-channel unification (Instagram, call, email).
5. Graduation flow into NuCRM/AbetFlow.

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Meta bundles this for free | Compete on depth (CRM, attribution, cross-channel), not on the bot |
| WhatsApp policy/quality throttling | Enforce opt-in, template quality, DMARC-style guardrails before send |
| Price pressure from cheap tools | Free tier to land; monetize the revenue engine, not the chat |
