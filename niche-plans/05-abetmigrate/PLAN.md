# AbetMigrate — Consolidation-as-a-Service

> **Product:** AbetMigrate · migrate.abetworks.in
> **Tier 2.5 · Strong adjacency** — Productizes the "seven subscriptions or one platform" consolidation pitch into a fixed-fee migration platform + practice.
> **Positioning:** "Leave your stack of six tools without breaking the quarter."

---

## 1. The problem

Abetworks' core sales argument is consolidation ($318/user across 7 tools → $142/user on the suite). But the barrier to switching is **migration risk**: field mapping, data validation, and the fear of breaking live sales while cutting over. That friction kills deals.

## 2. What AbetMigrate is

A **migration platform + productized service** that de-risks the switch:

1. **Stack audit** — connectors read the customer's current tools (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zapier, sequencers, helpdesks) and produce a consolidation report + savings model.
2. **Field-mapping engine** — maps source schema to the Abetworks one-data-model with validation and conflict resolution.
3. **Dual-run migration** — runs old + new in parallel, reconciles records, validates completeness before cutover.
4. **Rollback** — a tested rollback plan you hope not to need.
5. **Fixed-fee sprint** — the services team runs it as a defined-scope engagement.

## 3. Why Abetworks wins

Turns the biggest objection (switching cost) into a paid, low-risk on-ramp — and every migration lands a customer on the full suite. Leverages the existing services team and the published "leaving Salesforce" playbook.

## 4. Go-to-market

- **ICP:** mid-market teams (25–200 seats) on a fragmented 5–7 tool stack.
- **Wedge:** free stack audit + savings model → fixed-fee migration.
- **Pricing:** fixed-fee per migration tier (by seats + source complexity); credited against subscription.

## 5. Build phases

1. Source connectors (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zapier, Freshdesk/Zendesk, CSV).
2. Field-mapping + validation engine.
3. Dual-run reconciliation + cutover tooling.
4. Rollback + audit of every migrated record.
5. Savings-model report generator (feeds the consolidation calculator on the site).

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Dirty source data | Validation + reconciliation reports before cutover |
| Cutover breaks live sales | Dual-run + tested rollback |
| Connector maintenance load | Prioritize top 6 sources; CSV fallback for the long tail |
