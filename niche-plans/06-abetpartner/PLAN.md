# AbetPartner — White-Label Suite for Agencies & Consultancies

> **Product:** AbetPartner · partner.abetworks.in
> **Tier 2.6 · Strong adjacency** — Multi-client workspaces + white-labelled reporting so agencies resell Abetworks under their own brand.
> **Positioning:** "Run all your clients on one platform — under your brand."

---

## 1. The opportunity

Agencies and consultancies juggle many clients across many tools, re-buying software per client and stitching reports by hand. A white-label suite makes them resellers and deepens Abetworks' distribution — recurring, sticky revenue.

## 2. What AbetPartner is

1. **Multi-client workspaces** — isolated tenants per client under one partner account.
2. **White-labelled** — partner's brand, domain, logo, and email identity.
3. **Isolated sending domains** — each client's deliverability reputation is separate.
4. **Cross-client reporting** — permission-gated rollups + white-labelled client reports.
5. **Retainer profitability** — per-workspace usage metering and margin visibility.

## 3. Why Abetworks wins

The one-data-model + one-permission-system architecture makes hierarchical multi-tenancy natural. Agencies become a distribution channel that sells and supports on Abetworks' behalf.

## 4. Go-to-market

- **ICP:** digital/growth agencies, RevOps consultancies, marketing firms managing 5–100 clients.
- **Wedge:** "Stop re-buying tools per client. One platform, your brand, better margins."
- **Pricing:** partner platform fee + wholesale per-workspace pricing; partner sets retail.

## 5. Build phases

1. Hierarchical tenancy (partner → workspaces) with hard isolation.
2. White-label branding + custom domains (ACM).
3. Isolated sender identities per client.
4. Billing rollup + margin reporting.
5. White-labelled client report templates.

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Client-data leakage across workspaces | RLS + grant-scoped partner visibility + audit |
| Deliverability cross-contamination | Per-client sender identities |
| Channel conflict with direct sales | Clear partner vs direct segmentation + deal registration |
